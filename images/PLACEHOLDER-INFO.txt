====================================
LOVE HAUTE SPACES - IMAGE PLACEHOLDERS
====================================

This file explains where to place your actual business images.

REQUIRED IMAGES TO UPLOAD:
====================================

1. LOGO (MOST IMPORTANT!)
   File: logo.png
   Location: This folder (images/)
   What: Your official Love Haute Spaces logo
   Specs: PNG format, transparent background, 200x200px minimum

2. PRODUCT IMAGES
   Files needed in this folder (images/):
   - headboards.jpg (Photo of your upholstered headboards)
   - ottomans.jpg (Photo of your ottomans)
   - side-tables.jpg (Photo of your side tables)
   - drawers.jpg (Photo of chest of drawers)
   Specs: JPG format, 800x600px minimum, under 300KB each

3. CUSTOM BUILT-IN IMAGES
   Files needed in this folder (images/):
   - wardrobes.jpg (Custom wardrobe/closet project)
   - bathroom-vanity.jpg (Bathroom vanity project)
   - office-cabinets.jpg (Office cabinet project)
   - tv-stands.jpg (TV stand project)
   - kitchens.jpg (Custom kitchen project)
   Specs: JPG format, 1000x750px minimum, under 400KB each

4. GALLERY IMAGES
   Location: images/gallery/ subfolder
   Files: Add multiple project photos with these naming patterns:
   - headboard-1.jpg, headboard-2.jpg (for upholstered furniture)
   - kitchen-1.jpg, kitchen-2.jpg (for kitchen projects)
   - wardrobe-1.jpg, wardrobe-2.jpg (for wardrobe projects)
   - bathroom-1.jpg, bathroom-2.jpg (for bathroom projects)
   - cabinet-1.jpg, cabinet-2.jpg (for other custom cabinets)
   Specs: JPG format, 1200x900px, under 500KB each
   Add as many as you want!

5. OPTIONAL IMAGES
   - hero.jpg (Homepage background, 1920x1080px, under 800KB)
   - about.jpg (About section image, 800x600px, under 400KB)

====================================
WHAT HAPPENS IF IMAGES ARE MISSING?
====================================

The website is designed to handle missing images gracefully:
- Missing gallery images will show elegant placeholders
- Missing product images will display with a clean background
- The website will still function perfectly

However, for the BEST CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE, please upload:
1. Your actual logo (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
2. Real photos of your furniture products
3. Multiple gallery images of completed projects

====================================
HOW TO ADD IMAGES
====================================

SIMPLE METHOD:
1. Save your images with the exact filenames listed above
2. Copy them into this "images" folder
3. For gallery images, copy them into the "images/gallery" subfolder
4. Refresh your browser to see the changes

DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS:
See IMAGE-UPLOAD-GUIDE.md in the main project folder

====================================
QUICK START PRIORITY
====================================

If you're in a hurry, upload these first:
1. logo.png (Your business logo) - ESSENTIAL
2. 3-5 photos for the gallery folder - Shows your work
3. Product images - Makes sections look complete

You can always add more images later!
