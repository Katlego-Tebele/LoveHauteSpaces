## 📋 What You Need to Do (3 Simple Steps)

### ✅ STEP 1: Add Your Logo & Photos (15 minutes)

**Priority 1 - Logo:**
- Save your logo as `logo.png` or `logo.svg`
- Place it in the `images/` folder
- **This is the most important file to add!**

**Priority 2 - Product Photos:**
Add these files to the `images/` folder:
- `headboards.jpg` - Your upholstered headboards
- `ottomans.jpg` - Your ottomans  
- `side-tables.jpg` - Your side tables
- `drawers.jpg` - Chest of drawers
- `wardrobes.jpg` - Wardrobe project
- `kitchens.jpg` - Kitchen project
- `bathroom-vanity.jpg` - Bathroom vanity

**Priority 3 - Gallery Photos:**
- Add 10-20 project photos to `images/gallery/` folder
- Name them: `kitchen-1.jpg`, `wardrobe-1.jpg`, etc.

📖 **Detailed instructions:** See `IMAGE-UPLOAD-GUIDE.md`

---

### ✅ STEP 2: Test Your Website (5 minutes)

1. **Open the website:**
   - Double-click `index.html`
   - It will open in your web browser

2. **Check everything works:**
   - ✓ Navigation menu works
   - ✓ Your logo appears
   - ✓ Product images show correctly
   - ✓ Gallery images load
   - ✓ WhatsApp buttons work
   - ✓ Test on your phone (send yourself the file)

---

### ✅ STEP 3: Put it Online (10 minutes)

**Easiest Method - GitHub Pages (FREE):**

1. Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and sign up (free)
2. Create a new repository called `love-haute-spaces`
3. Upload ALL your website files
4. Enable GitHub Pages in Settings → Pages
5. Done! Your website is live at: `yourusername.github.io/love-haute-spaces`

📖 **Detailed instructions:** See `DEPLOYMENT-GUIDE.md`

**Other FREE options:**
- Netlify - Just drag and drop your files
- Vercel - Quick and easy
- Traditional web hosting - If you already have one

## 🆘 Common Questions

**Q: The website shows placeholder images?**  
A: That's normal! Add your real photos to the `images/` folder.

**Q: How do I change colors?**  
A: Edit `css/style.css` - colors are at the top in the `:root` section.

**Q: Can I add more products?**  
A: Yes! Copy-paste an existing product card in `index.html` and change the text.

**Q: How do I add more gallery photos?**  
A: Just drop more photos into `images/gallery/` folder. Use naming like `kitchen-5.jpg`, `wardrobe-3.jpg`.

**Q: The WhatsApp links don't work?**  
A: They work perfectly once online! Test after deploying.

**Q: Is it really free?**  
A: Yes! GitHub Pages, Netlify, and Vercel are 100% free forever.
